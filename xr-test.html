WebXR test

The smallest WebXR loop, apart from the 3Draft renderer: a WebGL2 context, an XRWebGLLayer and the session's own requestAnimationFrame. In the headset you should see a turning cube 1.5 m in front of you over a grid floor, the left eye on a dark red, the right eye on a dark blue background. The log below records the session, the framebuffer, both eyes' viewports and every WebGL error; it stays here after you leave VR (and in this browser's storage).
