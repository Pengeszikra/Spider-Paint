XR compare

The same scene twice: a turning teal cube 1.5 m ahead at eye height, a lying golden egg to the right, a floor grid and stars round you. three.js draws it with its own renderer and WebXRManager; 3Draft with the 3Draft engine's WebGL2 renderer (the viewer's), in the plainest WebXR loop. If three.js shows it and 3Draft does not, the fault is in the 3Draft renderer; if both show it, it is in the viewer around it. Leave VR with the Meta button or the left X; the log below (the newest line last) tells what happened. 3Draft's background is dark blue, three.js's near black.
